Questions people may ask

We will keep improving these answers as the platform grows.

Is the result a guarantee that my business will make money?

No. The result is a planning estimate. Real business results depend on location, prices, customers, competition, management and many other factors.

Does BiasharaCheck know the exact cost of every business?

Not yet. This first version uses simple estimates. Later we can connect verified local information and improve the estimates by business and location.

Do I have to pay to check my idea?

The basic business check is intended to be free. More detailed tools may be offered later.

Will BiasharaCheck ask for my M-Pesa PIN?

No. Never give your M-Pesa PIN to a website. Any future payment connection will be designed so that your PIN remains private.
